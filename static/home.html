This is a private photo album site for the Cole and Ullvang families. Invited members sign in to view albums of family photographs; the site is not open to the public.

Signing in with Google gives the site your email address and basic profile. The email address is used only to check it against the family invite list and decide whether to show you the albums. No other Google data is read, and nothing is written to your Google account.
